# Kifayat Content Plan — 8 Articles to Rank in 1–3 Months

This plan covers the first eight blog articles for kifayat.co. The goal is realistic: win long-tail searches ("best wireless earbuds under 5000 in Pakistan") that have clear buyer intent and low competition, build trust with shoppers, and feed internal links into category and product pages. Head terms are a 6–18 month game (see SEO-GETTING-INDEXED.md, section 6) — this plan targets the 1–3 month window.

## Before publishing — two prerequisites (flag these)

1. **Blog routes do not exist yet.** Articles below are written for URLs under `/blog/<slug>`, but those routes are not built. Nothing can be published until a developer adds blog routes. Good news: robots.txt already allows `/blog/`, so Google can crawl articles the moment they exist.
2. **Article/BlogPosting schema must ship with the blog.** The JSON-LD component already supports an Article schema; it is being wired in separately. Do not publish without it — the schema is what turns a plain article into rich results (author, date, headline) and is one of the cheapest ranking wins available.

## Article 1 — Electronics: best wireless earbuds under 5000 in Pakistan

- **Outline:**
  1. Why the under-Rs. 5,000 band is the sweet spot (what you get, what you give up)
  2. What to check at this price: battery life, charging case, mic quality, latency, warranty
  3. The 5 best picks — comparison table: model, price, battery, case, best for

- **Why it wins:** High buyer intent, low competition at the exact price point, and the comparison + table + FAQ format is exactly what Google shows as featured snippets.

## Publishing cadence and format

- **Cadence:** two articles per month → all eight live in about four months. Publish the first two as soon as blog routes exist, then hold the rhythm; the search data starts compounding after article 3–4.
- **Length:** 800–1,200 words each. Enough to be useful, short enough to actually finish.
- **Prices:** always real, current PKR prices. Verify each price on publish day. Price-anchored articles (1, 2, 3, 5) need a 15-minute price refresh every quarter — Pakistani prices move fast.
- **Format:** comparison tables for list articles, one clear H1, short sections with one idea each, and an FAQ block at the end of every article (a natural FAQPage schema opportunity for the FAQ page later).
- **Internal links:** every article gets 2–3 links to live category, product, or policy pages — never to articles that do not exist yet (use "once published" placeholders only during drafting).
- **One keyword per article.** Do not stuff multiple targets; each article owns one query.

### Pre-publish checklist (run for every article)

1. Unique title tag and meta description (keyword + price/benefit hook; title under ~60 characters).
2. Exactly one H1 on the page, matching the article title.
3. Alt text on every image (describe the product, include the keyword where natural).
4. 2–3 internal links to live pages; confirm the URLs resolve.
5. BlogPosting schema present and valid (use Google's Rich Results Test before publishing).
6. Article renders a real page at `/blog/<slug>` — no empty shell, no soft 404.
7. Once routes exist, confirm blog URLs are added to a sitemap so Google finds them quickly (ask the developer to include `/blog/` URLs in the sitemap output).

When in doubt, re-read the near-win and content-gap steps in SEO-GETTING-INDEXED.md — Search Console will tell you which of these articles is working within 4–8 weeks.
